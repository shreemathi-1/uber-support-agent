---
title: Payment method declined or not working
source_url: https://help.uber.com/en/riders
intent: app_or_account_issue
date_checked: UNVERIFIED-PLACEHOLDER
---
A payment method can be declined when the card has expired, the bank blocks the
transaction, or a previous trip has an outstanding balance. Riders can add or update a
payment method under Wallet in the app and settle any outstanding amount there. Some
cards require a small temporary authorisation hold, which is released automatically.
