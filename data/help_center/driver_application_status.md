title: Checking the status of a driver application or background check
source_url: https://help.uber.com/en/riders
intent: driver_onboarding_or_earnings
date_checked: UNVERIFIED-PLACEHOLDER
---
Driver applicants can see the status of each required step — documents, vehicle
inspection, and background screening — in the driver app or the partner website.
Background checks are run by a third-party provider and can take from a few days to
several weeks depending on location. If a step shows as pending for a long time, the
applicant should confirm all documents were uploaded clearly and are unexpired. Support
needs the email address used on the application to look up the file.
