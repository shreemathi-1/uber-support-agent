---
title: Why a cancellation fee was charged
source_url: https://help.uber.com/en/riders
intent: fare_dispute
date_checked: UNVERIFIED-PLACEHOLDER
---
A cancellation fee is charged when a rider cancels after the driver has already accepted
and a short grace period has passed, or when the driver arrives and the rider does not
show up within the waiting time. Fees vary by city. If the driver cancelled, or the driver
was clearly far from the pickup, riders can dispute the fee through Your Trips → select
the trip → Help → dispute the cancellation fee. Fees are typically waived when the driver
was at fault.
