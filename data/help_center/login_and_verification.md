---
title: Trouble signing in or not receiving verification codes
source_url: https://help.uber.com/en/riders
intent: app_or_account_issue
date_checked: UNVERIFIED-PLACEHOLDER
---
If a verification text does not arrive, riders can request the code again after a short
wait, check that the phone number on the account is correct, and try the option to
receive the code by call or email where offered. If the app shows repeated errors,
updating to the latest version, restarting the phone, or reinstalling often resolves it.
Accounts that appear locked or suspended require a review by support; the rider should
provide the email address on the account so the account can be located.
