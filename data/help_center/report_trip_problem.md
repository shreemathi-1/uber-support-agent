title: Reporting a problem with a driver or a trip

---
Problems with a specific trip — wrong route, unsafe driving, rude behaviour, vehicle
condition, a driver who did not show up — are reported from Your Trips → select the trip →
Help → report an issue with the driver or the ride. Riders can rate the driver and add a
comment; low ratings with comments are reviewed. Serious safety concerns should be
reported immediately and, in an emergency, riders should contact local emergency
services first. Uber may follow up by email and can adjust the fare when the route was
incorrect.
