title: Reviewing a fare or charge you don't recognise

A rider can ask for a fare review from inside the app rather than waiting for support.
Open the app, go to Your Trips, select the trip in question, tap Help, then choose the
option to review the fare or report a problem with the price. Common reasons include a
route that was longer than expected, a cancellation fee the rider believes was unfair,
surge pricing that was not shown clearly, or a trip that was started by mistake.
Uber reviews the request and, if an adjustment is made, refunds go back to the original
payment method and can take a few business days to appear. If the trip does not appear in
Your Trips, the charge may be a temporary authorisation hold rather than a completed fare.
