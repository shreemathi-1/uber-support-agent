title: Missing, wrong, or undelivered Uber Eats orders

Problems with a food order are reported from the Uber Eats app: open Orders, select the
order, and choose Help to report missing or incorrect items, a damaged order, or an order
that never arrived. Refunds or credits for eligible issues are applied to the original
payment method or as Uber Cash. Delivery availability depends on the city and the
restaurant's delivery radius; if an address is outside the area, the app will say so at
checkout.
