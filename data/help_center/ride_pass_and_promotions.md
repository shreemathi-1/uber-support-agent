Ride pass offers are not available to every rider; eligibility is selected by Uber and
shown in the app under the relevant section when available. Promo codes are entered
under Wallet or Promotions before requesting a ride and apply only to eligible trips
and cities. A promotion that did not apply to a completed trip can be reviewed through
the trip's Help section.
