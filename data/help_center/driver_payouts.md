title: Earnings, payouts and bank details

Earnings are paid out on a regular schedule to the bank account on file, and drivers can
review each payment statement in the driver app. Bank details can be updated in the app;
changes made after a payout has been issued do not redirect that payout. If a payout was
sent to a closed or incorrect account, the bank usually returns it and it is re-issued,
but this can take several business days and support may need to confirm the details.
