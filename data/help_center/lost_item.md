---
title: Recovering an item left in a vehicle
source_url: https://help.uber.com/en/riders
intent: lost_item
date_checked: UNVERIFIED-PLACEHOLDER
---
Riders can contact the driver directly through the app: Your Trips → select the trip →
Help → report a lost item. The app can place a call to the driver's phone without
revealing either number. If the driver cannot be reached, the rider can submit a lost
item report and support will follow up. A return fee may apply in some cities to
compensate the driver for their time.
