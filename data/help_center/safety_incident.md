If a rider feels unsafe or an incident occurs, the priority is personal safety: contact
local emergency services if needed. Inside the app, the Safety Toolkit on the trip screen
lets riders share their trip status with a contact and reach emergency help. Afterwards,
the incident should be reported through the trip's Help section. Safety reports are
handled by a dedicated team and are not resolved through automated replies.
