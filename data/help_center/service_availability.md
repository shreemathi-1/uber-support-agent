Uber operates in many cities but not all; the app shows available products at the
pickup location. Fares are calculated from an upfront price shown before the ride is
requested, based on estimated time, distance, and demand. When demand is high the price
may be higher and this is shown before confirming. Airport pickups may include an
airport surcharge set by the airport authority.
